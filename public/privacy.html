Privacy Notice

Eozilla does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or any other third-party tracking code.

The app stores only the last selected OGC API - Processes service and its configuration in your browser's localStorage so you do not have to enter it again on the next visit.

When you connect to a OGC API service, the app sends requests only to that provider. The app itself does not forward your data to our own servers.

If a result contains an external image, audio, video, or iframe URL, opening the preview loads that URL directly in your browser.

  • Storage purpose: remember the last selected OGC API service provider.
  • Storage location: your browser, via localStorage.
  • Retention: until you clear your browser data or remove it manually.

You can clear this storage in your browser settings at any time.
